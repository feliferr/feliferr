# Felipe Ferreira

**Machine learning researcher and engineer.** I work on multimodal video understanding, retrieval and
recommender systems, and on the platforms that put ML into production. Based in Brazil.

Currently leading an MLPlat and RecSys teams at Stone. Previously worked on recommender systems and multimodal
content understanding at Globo.

## What I work on

- **Video and audio-visual understanding:** temporal grounding and long-video QA, with a focus on
  efficient, training-free or parameter-efficient methods that run on modest hardware.
- **Multimodal retrieval and RAG:** hybrid search over speech, on-screen text, scene descriptions and
  frames, evaluated with recall@k and MRR.
- **Recommender systems:** offline-first evaluation (temporal splits, full ranking, beyond-accuracy
  metrics) before any learned model.
- **ML platforms and MLOps:** reproducible pipelines, CI, typed code and clear contracts between stages.

## Featured projects

- **[recsys](https://github.com/feliferr/recsys):** typed, tested toolkit for offline evaluation of
  recommenders, with temporal splits, full-ranking metrics and non-learned baselines. Learned models
  and serving are on the roadmap.
- **[research-kb](https://github.com/feliferr/research-kb):** an open knowledge base of what I read,
  study and prototype in applied AI (computer vision, NLP, RecSys, GenAI, MLOps), with a capture
  pipeline and weekly study plans.
